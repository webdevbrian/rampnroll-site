How do I throw?

Touch anywhere and the ball moves with your finger. Flick up the screen and let go: how fast you flick is how hard it goes, and the way your finger's moving is where it goes. Too hard is as bad as too soft: an overthrown ball hits the net and drops. Flick diagonally to bank a shot off the rails toward the corner 100s. A throw that's too weak to reach the hop rolls back to you: tap while it's rolling back to catch it and throw again.

With a game controller: slide the ball with the left stick or d-pad, then flick the right stick (pull back, snap forward), or hold A and let go when the meter's in the green. With a keyboard: A and D or the arrows slide the ball, and holding Space shows the meter. Every control is listed in Settings → Controls.

What do the lights mean?

Roll over all three lights on the lane and your next score is doubled. One ring pulses: it's the hot ring, and it pays double. Matching or beating your last ring builds a combo up to ×5. Hit 10, 20, 30, 40 and 50 in a round for a bonus, and a 100 (or four 40s or better) sets up multiball for your next throw. The jackpot grows with every ball and pays out on a 100 while the double's lit.

Prestige

At level 50 you can keep playing for as long as you like, or Prestige: your level and prizes reset, a new House Rule makes things a little meaner, and tickets pay better from then on. Your tickets, balls, lanes, achievements and high scores stay.

The lane packs and restoring purchases

Lights Out, Creature Feature and Bad Ideas are bought once and kept for good. To get them back on a new phone or after reinstalling, open Lanes (from the main menu) and tap Restore purchases at the bottom, or use Settings → Restore purchases. Use the same Apple Account or Google account you bought them with. On iPhone and iPad, packs aren't shared through Family Sharing.

The sound is off

Check the volumes in Settings. On an iPhone, the ring/silent switch mutes game sound. If the sound goes quiet after switching apps, tap the screen once.

The lights are too much

Turn on Settings → Reduce flashing: the lamps glow steadily instead of chasing and strobing. Screen shake can be turned off there too.

The words are too small

Settings → Text size makes every word and button bigger.

Starting over

Settings → Start over clears your progress and high scores (your settings stay). Lane packs you've bought stay yours: restore them afterwards.
