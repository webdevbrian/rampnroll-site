The short version

Ramp n Roll doesn't collect any information about you. There are no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking, and the game doesn't send anything over the internet. If you buy a lane pack, the App Store or Google Play handles it.

What the game stores

Your progress (your tickets, level, prizes, balls, lanes and achievements), your high scores and the initials you sign them with, and your settings are saved on your device, so you can pick up where you left off. They never leave your device, except as part of your device's own backups if you use them. Deleting the app deletes this data, and Settings → Start over in the game wipes your progress at any time.

Sharing a replay

When you share a replay, the game makes the clip or picture on your device and hands it to the share sheet or the app you choose; it doesn't upload anything itself. A share card shows the shot, the round's score, your level, and your three initials if you've signed the high scores. If you save a clip to your photos, your device may ask your permission to add it there; the game never reads your photos.

The lane pack purchases

The three lane packs are the game's only purchases, and they're optional. If you buy one, the App Store or Google Play handles the purchase, through Apple's or Google's own purchase system on your device: we have no server and no account of yours, and we never see your name, your store account or your payment details. Apple's or Google's privacy policy covers the purchase itself.

The game only asks the store, on your device, which packs belong to your store account, so it can unlock them (and restore them on a new device). It keeps nothing about a purchase but a note in its save on your device of the packs you have, so they work offline. Nothing about it is sent to us.

Information we collect

None. Ramp n Roll doesn't collect, sell or share personal information, and it doesn't include third-party analytics or advertising code.

Children

Ramp n Roll collects no information from anyone, children included.
