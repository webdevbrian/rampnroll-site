Flick a wooden ball up the lane. It hits the hop, sails into the rings and rattles round a hoop. Lights chase, the display yells at you, and a ribbon of paper tickets spits out of the machine. Spend them at the prize counter on ridiculous junk that makes you better. Then go again.

Flick it

Swipe to throw: how fast and which way you swipe is how hard and where it goes. Bank shots off the rails and sink the corner 100s.

Light it up

Rollovers, a hot ring, combos up to ×5, multiball and a jackpot. The last ball of every round goes slow-mo, and your best plays back like TV.

Win junk

Trade your tickets for 74 silly prizes, each a tiny buff that stacks, and 31 balls to roll, from a Sad Meatball to A Whole Potato.

What's in the machine

  • Seven lanes to play: Old Reliable, plus Pizza Parlor, Boardwalk, Bowling Alley Reject, Grandpa's Garage, Laundromat and Roller Rink, earned with tickets. Each has its own look, sounds, songs, a board that moves, visitors who wander by, and three hidden secrets.
  • A round is nine balls and about two minutes. One round, or a whole evening.
  • Climb to level 50, then Prestige if you like: the House Rules get meaner, and the tickets pay better.
  • 50 achievements and 54 lane secrets, and high score tables for every lane, with arcade initials.
  • A jukebox of 30 original songs, and two more for every lane.
  • Share your best shot as a short clip with sound, or as a picture card.
  • Reduce flashing, text size, and touch, keyboard or controller. Twelve languages.
  • No ads, no timers, no energy. No accounts, nothing collected, and it plays offline.

Free to play, and three lane packs

Ramp n Roll is free, with no ads. Seven lanes, all 74 prizes and 22 of the 31 balls are yours to earn by playing.

Three optional lane packs add wilder lanes and balls. Each is bought once, kept for good, and restorable on a new phone: Lights Out (Blacklight, Power Cut, Neon Drive), Creature Feature (The Spider, Swamp, Haunted Fairground, Church Fête) and Bad Ideas (Tiny Holes, Kitchen Sink, Bounce House, Office Party). They change looks and rules, never your score: every lane keeps its own high scores.
